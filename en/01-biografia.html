BIOGRAPHY

01.1 / JOURNEY

From a very young age there has always been a passion for capturing moments. It was thanks to an analogue camera, which recorded on Hi8, that I became a collector of moments, until I decided to create them.

Later, having explored the practice, I decided to learn the theory: I did a bachelor's degree and finished a master's in Cinema at the University of Beira Interior.

Cinema is an individual experience.

01.2 / EDUCATION AND METHOD

I have always dedicated myself to learning. Through various academic and personal projects, I have explored every facet of cinema: sound, photography, editing and directing.

I am very interested in the metaphor that exists in images, so I always seek to maximise the potential of cinematic language. I am meticulous with the object, not out of vanity but out of respect. Whatever the subject, the image should carry the feeling, not explain it.

01.3 / TOOLS

Video and animation: Vegas Pro, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Animate.
Photography and graphic design: Lightroom, Photoshop, Illustrator.
Sound design: Ableton, Audition.
