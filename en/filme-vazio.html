VAZIO

SYNOPSIS

Ego begins writing a film as a way of finding his identity. Accompanied by Conscience, he faces creative block, the instability of reality and the discovery that the story he is trying to write already exists. In Ego's despair, the film becomes the culmination of the creative process.
